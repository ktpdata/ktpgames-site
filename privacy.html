Privacy at KTP Games

Effective November 4, 2026. This policy covers every app published by KTP and Data, Inc. (KTP Games) and this website. Each app also has its own policy with the exact details for that app, linked below. Where an app's policy is more specific, it governs.

Our promise

We collect as little as possible, and in most of our apps that means nothing at all.

  • We do not show advertising in any of our apps.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, attribution, or tracking kits from other companies.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not let anyone else do it through our apps.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • No app requires your real name, email address, or location.
  • The two features that use our servers store only what their policies list, and you can have it deleted.

Apps that collect nothing

These apps send nothing to us. Everything stays on your device, or in your own private iCloud account where an app offers sync. We cannot see it, read it, or delete it.

  • Brain Drain Math
  • Brain Drain Vocab
  • AkiBreathe
  • AKI Cards
  • MemoryTide

Apps with an online feature

Two apps have a feature that needs a server. In both, the rest of the app works on your device, and only the data listed in that app's policy is stored, on Microsoft Azure servers in the United States that we operate.

Focus Syllabl: Collaborate (optional, off by default, ages 13 and up)
If you turn on group chat and shared tasks, we store the display name you choose, a random device ID and sign-in token, what you share with your groups, and a push notification token. You can delete your Collaborate account in the app at any time.
Moguls and Misers: leaderboard and achievements
We store the display name and avatar you choose, a random device identifier, your scores and achievements, and whether you have a premium purchase. You can hide yourself from the public leaderboard in the app, and we will delete your profile on request.

Permissions

Some apps ask for access to a device feature, such as Photos, Health, Calendars, Reminders, or Notifications, to provide something you asked for. We ask only when the feature is first used, the data is used on the device, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings. Saying no never locks you out of the rest of the app.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see, receive, or store your name, payment details, or billing address. Apple tells an app only whether a purchase or subscription is active. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Playing together

Where an app offers SharePlay, game moves travel between the people in the call through Apple's FaceTime service. They are not sent to us.

Children and families

Brain Drain Math and Brain Drain Vocab are made for children. They are built to collect no personal information, in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws elsewhere.

  • No advertising, no tracking, no chat, and nothing sent to us.
  • A parent gate stands in front of purchases, settings, and any link that leaves the app.
  • Each child's profile asks for as little as possible, such as a first name or nickname and a birth year, and stays on the family's devices and iCloud.
  • Parents can delete a child's profile, and everything recorded for it, from inside the app.

Features that store data on our servers are not offered in our children's apps. Focus Syllabl's Collaborate feature is for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email support@ktpdata.com and we will delete it.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They contain no personal information, and we use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

When you email us

If you write to support@ktpdata.com, we use your email address and message only to answer you. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your message. Ask us and we will delete the conversation.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking scripts, and it loads no fonts or code from other companies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those records.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA give you the right to access, correct, and delete personal information a company holds about you, and to object to how it is used. For the apps that collect nothing, we hold nothing, and you control your data directly: delete it in the app, delete the app, or remove its data from iCloud in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. For Focus Syllabl Collaborate and the Moguls and Misers leaderboard, use the in-app controls or email support@ktpdata.com and we will respond within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If any app ever starts collecting something new, we will say so here, in that app's policy, and in the App Store privacy label before the change ships.
