About

KTP Games is an independent studio based in Arizona. We build apps entirely on Apple's own frameworks, with privacy as a default rather than an afterthought.

KTP Games is a trade name of KTP and Data, Inc., a Delaware corporation.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026. This policy applies to all apps published by KTP and Data, Inc. (KTP Games).

What we collect

Our apps do not collect personal data. We do not run advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

Where your data lives

Content you create in our apps is stored on your device. Where an app offers sync, it uses your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access.

Permissions

Some apps ask for access to features such as Photos, Location, or Health. That access is used only on your device to provide the feature you requested, and you can change it at any time in Settings.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
