Privacy is the product

Most apps say they care about privacy. Ours mostly can't violate it, because there's nothing to collect.

No ads, no analytics

No ad networks, no tracking SDKs, no third-party code. Not on the apps, not on this site.

Five apps send nothing

Brain Drain Math, Brain Drain Vocab, AkiBreathe, AKI Cards, and MyMemoryTide never contact our servers. Data stays on your device or in your own iCloud.

Two apps, one feature each

Focus Syllabl Collaborate and the Moguls and Misers leaderboard are the only online features. Their policies list exactly what is stored, and you can delete it.

Apple handles money

Purchases and subscriptions go through Apple. We never see your payment details. Children's apps keep a parent gate in front of purchases, settings, and outside links.
