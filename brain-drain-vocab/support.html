Brain Drain Vocab Support

Brain Drain Vocab is vocabulary training that keeps your progress on your device and in your iCloud.

Get help

Found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest a feature? Email us and include the app name, your device model, and its iOS or Android version.

support@ktpdata.com

Common questions

I forgot the parent PIN. On the PIN screen, tap Forgot PIN and confirm you are the device's owner with Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode (on Android, the device's fingerprint, face, or screen lock). Then choose a new PIN. We can't reset it for you: we have no server and never see your PIN.

A learner forgot their PIN. Learner PINs are optional. A parent can change or remove any learner's PIN in the parent area.

The app went back to the learner list by itself. On a device shared by two or more learners, the app returns to the list of learners after the learner stops using it for 2 minutes. The learner's place is kept. A parent can change this to 1 or 5 minutes, or Never, in the parent area.

Progress isn't showing on my other iPhone or iPad. Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account, with iCloud allowed for Brain Drain Vocab in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Open the app on each device and give it a minute to sync. Android devices don't sync.

Subscriptions. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings (iPhone and iPad) or in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions (Android). Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.
