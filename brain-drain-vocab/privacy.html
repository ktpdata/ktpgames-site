Brain Drain Vocab Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026. This policy covers Brain Drain Vocab, published by KTP and Data, Inc. (KTP Games). It adds app-specific detail to the KTP Games privacy policy.

The short version

Brain Drain Vocab does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything your family does in the app stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, where we cannot see it. The app talks only to Apple: iCloud for sync and the App Store for subscriptions.

This policy is written to be read by a parent, not a lawyer.

What the app stores

WhatWhy
A display name, a built-in avatar, and a birth year for each learner (up to six)Tells learners apart on the "Who's learning?" picker. A nickname works fine. There are no photos.
Placement results and current levelStarts each learner at the right level
Answers: which question, which choice, right or wrong, and how long it tookBrings missed words back, decides when a learner can move up, and powers the stats page
Streaks, sessions, and level historyShown on the learner's stats page and the parent's family view
Reminder settings: on or off, time, days, and which device ringsSo a reminder rings on one device, not every device in the house
The parent PIN and any profile PINs, stored only as scrambled (hashed) valuesKeeps children out of settings and out of each other's profiles. The actual digits are never stored.

The app does not ask for, and has no way to record, an email address, phone number, full name, school, location, contacts, or photos. The vocabulary words and questions are built into the app; nothing is generated online.

Where it is kept

On the device, and synced between your family's devices through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit private database. That copy belongs to your iCloud account. We cannot read it, and it never reaches us. If no one is signed in to iCloud, the data stays on that single device and the app says so.

Permissions

The app asks for one permission, and only if a parent turns on reminders: Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on the device itself, never sent from a server, and never ring between 9 p.m. and 7 a.m. You can turn them off in the app or in Settings at any time. The app does not use the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, or health data.

Children

Brain Drain Vocab is made for children and families and is built to collect no personal information.

  • No advertising, no chat, no sharing, and no links to other apps on screens children use.
  • The parent PIN guards adding or deleting profiles, the family stats view, reminder settings, the subscription screen, and any link that leaves the app.
  • Deleting a profile permanently erases that learner's answers and progress on every device synced to your iCloud.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Subscriptions and payments

Training is available with an auto-renewing subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see, receive, or store your name, payment details, or billing address. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active for the Apple Account on the device. The subscription screen sits behind the parent PIN. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not track learners or let anyone else do so.
  • We do not show advertising, and never will.
  • We do not include analytics, crash-reporting, or any third-party software kits.
  • We do not sell, share, or disclose data, because we do not have any.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports, which we use only to fix bugs.

Deleting your data

  • One learner: delete the profile in the app (parent PIN required). This removes it from every synced device.
  • Everything on one device: delete the app.
  • Everything in iCloud: Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Brain Drain Vocab > Delete Data.

Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete personal information a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request from us. You control all of it on your devices and in your iCloud.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, we will say so here and in the App Store privacy label before it ships.
