Focus Syllabl Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026. This policy covers Focus Syllabl, published by KTP and Data, Inc. (KTP Games).

What we collect

Your tasks, habits, and plans are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud account. We cannot access them. We do not run advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share data.

Groups & Chat (optional)

If you choose to use Groups & Chat, the app creates an account on our server (hosted on Microsoft Azure) so you can share with other people. That account stores:

  • The display name and avatar you choose, and a random device identifier
  • The groups you create or join and their members
  • Messages, shared tasks, documents, and files you post to a group
  • A push notification token, so you can be notified of new messages

This content is visible to the members of the groups you share it with. It is not used for advertising or shared with anyone else. Files you upload can be set to expire after a number of days.

Deleting your data: you can delete individual documents and files, leave groups, or delete your Groups account entirely from within the app. Deleting your account removes it from our server. You can also email us to request deletion.

Permissions

Calendars and Reminders — to show and create events and reminders alongside your plans. This data is read on your device and is not sent to our server.

Notifications — used for reminders you set up. Scheduled on your device; you can turn them off in Settings.

iCloud and sync

Your personal data syncs between your devices through your own private iCloud account. Widgets read it through a container shared only within the app.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only which purchases are active.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@ktpdata.com
