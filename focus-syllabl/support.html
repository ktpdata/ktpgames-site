Want your college supported?

Focus Syllabl can import a syllabus and turn it into classes, assignments, and tasks. If your school's syllabi follow a consistent format from class to class, we may be able to add support for your school:

  1. Collect syllabi from a few of your classes (the more alike, the better).
  2. Email them to us with your school's name in the message.
  3. We'll review the format and let you know if we can add it.

Common questions

Reminders aren't arriving. Check that notifications are on in the app's Settings, and that Focus Syllabl is allowed to notify you in iOS Settings → Notifications. Quiet hours in the app can also hold reminders.

Where is my data stored? Your tasks, habits, classes, and schedule stay on your device, and deleting the app deletes them. Only what you share through Collaborate is stored on our servers — see our privacy policy.

How do I delete my Collaborate account? In the app, go to Settings → Collaborate → Delete Collaborate Account. This deletes your messages and uploaded files and removes you from every group. Your own tasks and habits on the device aren't affected.

How do I report someone in Collaborate? Long-press the message, file, note, or person and choose Report. You can also block anyone, which hides everything they post from you. You can email us too.

Calendar events aren't showing. Grant calendar access when prompted, then pick which calendars to show. Access is read-only.
