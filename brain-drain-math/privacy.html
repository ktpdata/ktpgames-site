Brain Drain Math Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. This policy covers Brain Drain Math, published by KTP and Data, Inc. (KTP Games).

The short version

Brain Drain Math does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own. Everything a child does in the app stays on the device.

This policy is written to be read by a parent, not a lawyer.

Who we are

KTP and Data, Inc. (KTP Games) publishes Brain Drain Math. If you have a question about this policy, contact us at support@ktpdata.com.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following on your device only:

None of it is transmitted anywhere. It is not backed up to our servers, because we do not operate any. If the app is deleted, this data is deleted with it. The PINs are the one exception in how that happens: iOS keeps Keychain items when an app is deleted, so the app erases any old PINs itself the first time it is opened after being installed again.

The name can be anything you like — a nickname works just as well. Beyond what is in the table, the app does not ask for, and has no way to record, an email address, photograph, location, contacts, or a date of birth beyond the birth year used to pick a difficulty level.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not track users, and we do not allow anyone else to.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not add analytics or crash-reporting services to the app.
  • We do not use third-party software development kits.
  • We do not sell, share, or disclose data, because we do not have any.
  • We do not build advertising profiles, and we never will for a children's app.

Children

This app is intended for children and is designed accordingly. Because we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing for us to disclose, share, or delete on request.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Parents stay in control on the device too: adding and removing a child's profile both sit behind the parent PIN, and removing one permanently deletes that child's progress from the device.

Parent PIN, player PINs and Face ID

Purchases, settings, and any link that leaves the app are protected by a 6-digit parent PIN that you choose. The PIN is stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value in this device's secure Keychain. It is never synced, never sent to us, and never shown in the app. After several wrong tries, entry pauses for a short time, and the pause grows with repeated wrong tries.

If you forget the PIN, you can reset it by confirming you are the device's owner with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. That check is performed entirely by iOS. The app receives only a yes or no answer and never has access to your face, fingerprint, or passcode. Face ID is used for nothing else in the app.

A parent can also set an optional 4-digit player PIN for any child, so that one child cannot switch to another child's profile. Player PINs are stored the same way as the parent PIN: only as a scrambled (hashed) value in this device's secure Keychain, never synced and never sent to us. A player PIN unlocks only that child's profile. It never opens purchases, settings, or anything else the parent PIN protects, and a parent can always get past it with the parent PIN.

Subscriptions and payments

Brain Drain Math Premium is available as an auto-renewing subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see, receive, or store your name, payment-card details, or billing address — Apple tells the app only whether an active subscription exists for the Apple Account signed in on the device.

Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The parent PIN stands in front of the paywall, so a child cannot reach a purchase unaided.

Diagnostic logging

The app writes diagnostic messages to the device's own system log to help with troubleshooting. These stay on the device, are not transmitted to us, and any value that could identify a player is marked private so it is redacted. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports, which we use only to fix bugs.

Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete personal information a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request. You can erase a single child's data with the in-app removal control, or erase everything by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, we will say so here clearly and in the App Store privacy label before it ships.
