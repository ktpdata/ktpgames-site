Brain Drain Math Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026. This policy covers Brain Drain Math, published by KTP and Data, Inc. (KTP Games).

The short version

Brain Drain Math does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own. Everything a child does in the app stays on the device.

This policy is written to be read by a parent, not a lawyer.

Who we are

KTP and Data, Inc. (KTP Games) publishes Brain Drain Math. If you have a question about this policy, contact us at support@ktpdata.com.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following on your device only:

WhatWhy
A first name and birth year you enter for each childTells children apart on the player picker and sets the difficulty and reading level of the questions
Game progress, levels and scoresSo a child can pick up where they left off
Mastery and accuracy recordsPowers the progress reports
Gold and unlocked characters in Maze RunnerPart of that game
Accessibility preferencesRemembers your colourblind display settings

None of it is transmitted anywhere. It is not backed up to our servers, because we do not operate any. If the app is deleted, this data is deleted with it.

The name can be anything you like — a nickname works just as well. Beyond what is in the table, the app does not ask for, and has no way to record, an email address, photograph, location, contacts, or a date of birth beyond the birth year used to pick a difficulty level.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not track users, and we do not allow anyone else to.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not use third-party software development kits.
  • We do not sell, share, or disclose data, because we do not have any.
  • We do not build advertising profiles, and we never will for a children's app.

Children

This app is intended for children and is designed accordingly. Because we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing for us to disclose, share, or delete on request.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Parents stay in control on the device too: removing a child's profile sits behind a parental gate, and doing so permanently deletes that child's progress from the device.

Subscriptions and payments

Full access is available as an auto-renewing subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see, receive, or store your name, payment-card details, or billing address — Apple tells the app only whether an active subscription exists for the Apple Account signed in on the device.

Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

A parental gate stands in front of the paywall, so a child cannot reach a purchase unaided.

Diagnostic logging

The app writes diagnostic messages to the device's own system log to help with troubleshooting. These stay on the device, are not transmitted to us, and any value that could identify a player is marked private so it is redacted. If you send a diagnostic report to Apple, that goes to Apple under their policy, not to us.

Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete personal information a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request. You can erase a single child's data with the in-app removal control, or erase everything by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, we will say so here clearly and in the App Store privacy label before it ships.
