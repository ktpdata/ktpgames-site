Brain Drain Math Support

Twelve math and logic games with levels that grow with each player.

Get help

Email us at support@ktpdata.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. Please include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

The Parent Area

Everything a parent manages lives in one place. On the home screen, tap Parent Area and enter your Parent PIN. Inside you'll find your subscription, your players, your Parent PIN, and links to this page and the privacy policy.

Players

How do I add another player?

Open the Parent Area, go to Players, and tap Add Player. Type a first name, pick an age, and tap Start. A name is required, and a nickname is fine. Each player gets their own levels, scores, and progress in every game.

How do we switch between players?

Tap a name in the "Who's playing?" row on the home screen. When there is more than one player, the app asks who's playing each time it starts, and again when you come back after two minutes or more away. It never interrupts a game in progress.

How do I remove a player?

Open the Parent Area, go to Players, and tap Remove on that player, then Remove again to confirm. Removing a player permanently deletes their levels, stats, and progress from the device. Players can only be removed from the Parent Area, so a child can't remove a profile from the home screen.

Can I stop one child from playing on another child's profile?

Yes. In the Parent Area, under Players, turn on Require PIN for a player and choose a 4-digit PIN. From then on, that PIN is asked for before anyone can switch to that player. Use Change PIN on the same row to change it, or turn the switch off to remove it.

My child forgot their player PIN.

On the PIN screen, tap Ask a parent and enter the Parent PIN to let them in. To set a new one, open the Parent Area, go to Players, and tap Change PIN. After several wrong tries the screen pauses for a minute.

Parent PIN

What is the Parent PIN?

A 6-digit PIN you choose the first time you open the app. It protects the Parent Area, purchases, and every link that leaves the app.

I forgot my Parent PIN.

On the Parent PIN screen, tap Forgot PIN? and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. You can then choose a new PIN.

How do I change my Parent PIN?

Open the Parent Area and tap Change Parent PIN.

Premium

What is free, and what does Premium add?

Without a subscription, a player can play one level of one game each day. Brain Drain Math Premium unlocks unlimited play in all twelve games, Water Sort, Circuit Mode, the full progress reports, and the reward world.

How do subscriptions work?

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple Account, monthly or yearly. The current price and any free trial are shown in the app before you buy. Open the Parent Area and go to Subscription to subscribe, to see which plan you're on, or to tap Manage Subscription to change or cancel it. Family Sharing is supported.

I subscribed on another device. How do I get Premium here?

Open the Parent Area, go to Subscription, and tap Restore Purchases.

Reward world

What is the reward world?

As a player finishes games, they build up a world of their own, one piece at a time: a Space Station or an Aquarium. A player opens theirs from the Station or Aquarium button at the top left of the home screen.

How do I choose between the Space Station and the Aquarium?

Open the Parent Area, go to Players, and choose a Reward world for each player. Switching worlds keeps their progress.

Can I turn the reward world off?

Yes. Turn off Show reward world, either in the Parent Area or under Accessibility on the home screen. Progress is kept, and games keep adding to it quietly, so nothing is lost if you turn it back on.

Privacy

Is our data safe?

Yes. The app collects nothing: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device. Read our privacy policy for details.

What happens if I delete the app?

All players and progress are deleted with it. If you install it again, the app starts fresh and asks you to choose a new Parent PIN. An active subscription is not affected; use Restore Purchases to pick it up again.
